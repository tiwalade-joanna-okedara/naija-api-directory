# Contributing

Thanks for helping other builders in Nigeria.

1. Edit `services.json`. Each service has `name`, `what`, `status` and `note`.
2. `status` is one of: `works`, `slow`, `paid`, `closed`, `partner`, `unreliable`, or empty.
3. Keep notes factual and first-hand: what you tried, when, and what happened. No adverts or affiliate links.
4. Open a pull request with a one-line summary of your experience.

Not sure? Open an issue instead and describe what you saw.
